Capabilities

What Sentinel gives a control room

Live Ops

Every camera on a live map, real-time alerts, and cross-camera vehicle-plate tracking with a searchable watchlist — the operator’s main screen.

Camera Health

Which cameras are live, faulty or unreachable right now, with the last time each was actually checked — never a guessed uptime number.

Incident Reports

Area-wise, week-by-week counts of what Sentinel itself has detected — watchlist hits, women’s-safety flags, SOS calls — grouped by district for planning and review.

Women’s Safety Map

A curated overlay of areas flagged by police judgement — not an algorithm — so patrol planning and the public-safety view share one honest picture.
The differentiator

Detect once. Route to the right responder, automatically.

When a camera recognises an incident, Sentinel doesn’t just raise an alert on a screen — it relays the location to the nearest responding unit first, then escalates to the state control room. The type of incident decides who gets it.

Crime / women’s safety
Camera → nearest PCR van → control room
Accident detected
Camera → nearest hospital → control room
Fire detected
Camera → nearest fire station → control room

Routing runs on demo responder locations today; on selection it connects to the department’s real PCR, hospital and fire-station rosters.

Principles

Built to be honest about its own data

No invented crime statistics

Incident Reports aggregate this platform’s own alert history. Sentinel has no source of official Gujarat crime data and does not fabricate one.

No invented uptime

Camera Health shows the last real probe for every camera. A camera that has never been checked says so — never a manufactured percentage.

Curated, not algorithmic, safety zones

Women’s-safety areas are entered and reviewed by police, labelled as curated judgement, and never presented as measured crime data.

Scoped by jurisdiction, always

Every read is scoped to what a signed-in officer’s jurisdiction and role permit — enforced at the database level, not just in the interface.
