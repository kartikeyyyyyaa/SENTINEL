        Raise an SOS

        Data & Privacy

        What is collected, and why

        Camera metadata, number-plate reads and watchlist matches are logged against a stated purpose and case reference. Raw video is never stored by Sentinel — it is only ever proxied live, on demand. Full detail: docs/DATA_HANDLING_AND_RETENTION.md

        Automated matching

        Plate and face matching are automated; opening an alert is automated. Dispatching, closing or acting on an alert is always a human decision, recorded under the operator’s own identity. Full detail: docs/AUTOMATED_PROCESSING_DISCLOSURE.md

        Access control & audit

        Every access is scoped by jurisdiction at the database level and written to an append-only, tamper-evident audit trail. Full detail: docs/SECURITY.md
